PlanByCam Privacy Policy

PlanByCam (package com.planbycam.app) for Android, published by Phonon Interactive

Effective date: 3 October 2026

In short. PlanByCam has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and we run no servers that receive your data. Your plans, photos and measurements are stored on your phone. The camera and motion sensors are used on the phone to measure. Your location is used only if you tap “Use my location”, and is then sent to your phone’s address lookup service to find the address. On supported devices the app uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google. Files leave your phone only when you choose to share, export, print or save them.

1. Who we are

PlanByCam is an Android app that measures distances, surfaces and heights with the phone’s camera and turns them into dimensioned floor plans. It is published by Phonon Interactive (“we”, “us”). Phonon Interactive is responsible for the app and for this policy. You can reach us about privacy at phonon.support@gmail.com.

This policy covers the PlanByCam app only. Other apps and services you use together with it (for example the app you share a plan with, Google Play, or Android itself) have their own privacy policies.

2. What the app uses, and why

PlanByCam works on your phone. It does not send the data below to us.

Camera

The camera is used only while the measuring screen is open, to show the live image and to track the phone’s position so that the points you place can be measured. The camera image is processed on the phone. It is not recorded as video and not uploaded by PlanByCam. If you take a scene photo on the measuring screen, that photo is saved with the plan on your phone. The camera permission is asked for when you first measure (or in the introduction); you can draw plans by hand without it.

Motion and other sensors

While you measure, the app reads the phone’s motion and orientation sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, rotation and magnetic field sensors) to follow how the phone is held and to draw the compass, and the light sensor to switch the torch on in dark rooms. These readings are used on the phone in real time. Only the plan’s compass direction (which way is north) is saved with the plan, and it leaves the phone only inside files you choose to share or export.

Plans, photos and notes

Everything you create in the app is stored in the app’s private storage on your phone: plans and measurements, room and plan names, folders, the address and notes you add to a plan, scene photos, and your settings. Files you import (PlanByCam backups or plan files you pick) are read only to add the plans they contain.

Location (optional)

The app asks for location access only when you tap Use my location in a plan’s details, to fill in the plan’s address. See Address lookup below. Location is never used in the background and is not needed for measuring.

Storage access on older phones

On Android 7 to 9 the app asks for storage access only to save an image to your gallery when you choose Save to gallery. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.

Vibration

The app uses vibration for short haptic feedback while measuring. This involves no personal data.

3. Address lookup (“Use my location”)

When you tap Use my location and allow location access, PlanByCam asks your phone for its current location (or a recent one) once. It then asks your phone’s built-in address lookup service (Android’s geocoder) to turn these coordinates into a street address.

  • On most phones this address lookup service is provided by Google (as part of Google Play services), so the coordinates are sent to Google to find the address. On some phones a different provider, chosen by the phone’s manufacturer, may do this. The provider handles the request under its own privacy policy; for Google, that is the Google Privacy Policy. How the phone finds its location (for example with GPS, Wi-Fi or mobile networks) depends on your phone’s location settings.
  • We never receive your location. PlanByCam does not store the coordinates: it keeps only the address that is filled in, which you can edit or delete. If no address is found, the coordinates themselves are filled in as the address, and are kept with the plan only if you save it that way.
  • This happens only when you tap the button. You can always type the address instead, deny location access, or turn it off later in Android’s settings.

4. Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

On devices that support it, PlanByCam uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) to track the phone’s position from the camera image and motion sensors while you measure. ARCore processes the camera image on the device; PlanByCam does not use any ARCore feature that uploads camera images or location (such as Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API). According to Google, ARCore collects information such as device identifiers, information about how the ARCore features are used, and performance and diagnostic data, and sends it to Google for analytics and to improve ARCore. This data goes to Google, not to us, and Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. See also Google’s page How Google Play Services for AR handles your data.

If your device does not support ARCore, or Google Play Services for AR is not installed, the app uses its own sensor-only tracking, which runs entirely on the phone.

5. What we don’t do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own; PlanByCam does not send usage data to us.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone. We have no copy of it to share.
  • No contacts, microphone, call, SMS or background location access.

6. Sharing, exporting, printing and backups

Plans and photos leave your phone only when you choose to:

  • Share or export a plan (for example as an image, PDF, CAD drawing, table, data file or PlanByCam backup): the file goes to the app or place you pick (email, messaging, cloud storage and so on), and that app or service handles it under its own policy. The shared text can include the plan name, address, areas and wall lengths, and plan notes only if you turn that option on.
  • Save to gallery: the image is saved to your phone’s shared pictures, where other apps you have allowed (such as a photo backup app) can see it.
  • Print: the plan goes to the printing service you choose in Android’s print dialog.

The app’s data is excluded from Android’s cloud backup. When you set up a new phone with a direct phone-to-phone transfer, Android 12 and later may still copy the app’s data to the new phone; this stays between your devices. To keep a copy of a plan, export it as a “PlanByCam backup (with photos)”.

7. Purchases

PlanByCam is a paid app sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; we never receive your card or bank details. Google gives us limited order information (for example an order number, the date, the price and the country or region), which we use only for accounting, tax and handling refunds. Google Play may also give us aggregated statistics and crash reports from users who chose to share usage and diagnostic data with Google; these do not identify you to us.

8. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and keep the conversation only as long as needed for that (and any legal obligation). Please do not send us plans or photos you want to keep private unless you need help with them.

9. Storage and security

Your plans and photos are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read, and are protected by your phone’s own security, such as the screen lock and device encryption. Files you export are stored where you choose. Because we hold no copy of your data, a problem on our side cannot expose it; please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Plans, photos and settings stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app’s data in Android’s settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on the phone, except files you exported or saved to the gallery yourself.
  • We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing we can restore if the data is lost.
  • Data collected by Google through ARCore or the address lookup is kept by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, which also explains your choices for it.
  • Emails and order records are kept as described in sections 7 and 8.

11. Your choices and rights

You can allow or deny the camera, location and storage permissions, and change them at any time in Android’s settings; measuring needs the camera, while everything else in the app works without it. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you and to object to its use. The only personal data we may hold are emails you send us and limited order information from Google Play; write to phonon.support@gmail.com to use these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Children

PlanByCam is a measuring tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes, we will update this policy on this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
